#131 (epic #126, 2026-11-17) added the picture gestures to the touch player (#130's chrome).

**Layout:**
- `frontend/src/lib/player-gestures.ts`: a pure `reduce(state, points, event, ctx)` → `{state, points, out}`. Zones are left 40 % brightness, right 40 % volume and the centre; dead zones are 24 pt from the top/bottom plus the safe-area insets (pans only). The direction locks after 10 pt (1.5× ratio). A `tick` event drives the 500 ms hold; a second finger is a pinch (fill > 1.15, original < 0.87). Seek: full width = min(90 s, duration), first 40 pt at ¼ speed, release within 10 pt of the start cancels. `tap` out is ignored; the surface's `onClick` still runs `tapPicture`, so old click tests keep working, and `gesture.current.notTap` swallows the click after a pan, hold or pinch.
- `player-page.tsx`: `feed`/`actOn`/`pointer` before `scrubbing`. `gesturesOn` = touchLayout, not locked, no sheet, no failure. Insets come from a hidden `insetProbe` div padded with `env()`. The level HUD state is `hud`. Hold calls `Player.SetSpeed(2)` and restores `heldSpeed` (not a saved choice). Double taps: `addSkip` runs (600 ms window, one seek 400 ms after the last tap, through `seekRef`/`positionRef`); a centre double tap toggles pause, so the centre single tap now waits 280 ms like the sides. `skipBy` = `settings.gestures.doubleTapSkip` on touch (desktop keeps `skip`). Lock: `locked` → `lockedNow` gates `shown`, the prompts and the resume pill; a tap shows `LockPill` for 2 s, and a 1 s hold unlocks. "Report a problem" in `TouchMoreMenu` = `Diagnostics.ShareLogs` (iOS).
- `touch-chrome.tsx`: `LevelHud` (material-media; there is no `glass-media-strong` since the revamp), `SkipRipple`, `HoldPill`, `LockPill`.
- Go: `Player.SetBrightness/Brightness/SetSystemVolume/SystemVolume/SetImmersive/SetPanscan` (desktop no-ops in `internal/player/touch.go`, iOS forwards to the engine through the `touchControls` interface in `service_ios.go`). Event `player:system-volume` (float64) is wired in `UseEngine` via `OnSystemVolume`. `iosplayer/device.go` has the cgo calls (it was already a cgo file, so the boundary file list is unchanged); `host_ios.m` has the brightness save/restore (resign-active restores, active again in the player re-applies), the hidden `MPVolumeView` (added at attach, removed at detach, -2000,-2000 at alpha 0.01) plus KVO, and the immersive swizzles (`class_replaceMethod` on `WailsViewController`, the old IMP kept). `Engine.Stop` restores brightness. `rotatingEngine` now always wraps (`phone` flag) and turns immersive on at Load and off at Stop on every device.
- **Haptics:** `internal/haptics` `Service.Impact(kind)` with kinds `light|medium|heavy|selection`, bound in `main.go` on every platform (desktop no-op). #135 extends this service rather than adding another.
- Store: `ProfileSettings.Gestures` {levelSwipes, seekSwipe, holdForSpeed, doubleTapSkip ∈ 5/10/15/30}, defaulted when empty in `SaveProfileSettings`; `TitleAdjustments.Fill *bool` (the pinch per title). `test/fixtures.ts` has `gestures`. Settings › Playback › Gestures shows on `isIOS()` (`GestureSettings` in routes/settings.tsx).

**Not verified (no simulator or device for this ticket):** MPVolumeView suppressing the system HUD, the KVO events, brightness restore after a call or the lock button, the edge deferral, pinch/panscan on a real film, and VoiceOver. VoiceOver is "off" only because the picture is aria-hidden, with no native check. Tests for the reducer and the page are owed.
